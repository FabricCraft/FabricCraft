# Code of Conduct

We welcome participants regardless of background or experience. Be respectful, constructive, and patient. Harassment, discriminatory abuse, threats, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

Maintainers may remove abusive content, moderate discussions, and restrict participation. A confidential maintainer contact for conduct reports is pending. For abuse on GitHub, use [GitHub’s reporting tools](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).

This is an interim project policy; the community may adopt the full Contributor Covenant after establishing an appropriate confidential reporting channel.
